## Purpose

We are evaluating a prototype that estimates whether a camera presentation is live and whether it matches an enrolled identity. The prototype is not a production access-control system. Participation is voluntary and is not connected to employment, grades, building access, or services.

## What participation involves

If you agree, the team will ask you to complete a short capture session under varied lighting and camera conditions. The session may include ordinary face captures and presentation-attack test conditions described to you before capture. You may skip any condition or stop at any time without giving a reason.

## Data collected and use

The team may collect face images/video, derived model scores, decision outcomes, timing measurements, and a random study code. Face imagery is sensitive biometric data. It will be used only for this project evaluation, access limited to named team members, stored encrypted, and deleted by **[specific deletion date or retention period]**. Reports will use aggregate results and will not identify participants. We will not upload study data to public repositories or use it to identify you outside this study.

## Risks and limits

Face data cannot be made anonymous in the same way as ordinary survey answers. There is a residual risk of unauthorized access or re-identification. Prototype decisions can be wrong and must not be used to make consequential decisions about you. Participation does not guarantee that the system recognizes or rejects any condition correctly.
